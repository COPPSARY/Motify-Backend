# Founder-story SaaS promo

Turn founder pain into a product journey: escalating editorial statements, tangible cost, confident product reveal, then instruction becoming workspace.

- Keep problem copy on uncluttered frames and use one physical metaphor per pain, never floating cards behind every sentence.
- Keep one continuity shell from prompt to preview to brand token; its faces share a coordinate system.
- Use purposeful interaction push-ins and payoff pullbacks; reserve high-energy colour shifts for problem-to-solution turns.

Use `macroSettle`, `growAndComplete`, and `morph` for editorial claims and product transformation.

## Code authoring contract

Create one `problemStage` for pure copy, one `morphShell` for prompt-to-workspace transformation, and one unboxed `ctaStage` for the close.
Use explicit actors for the cost metaphor, cursor, typed prompt, generate button, product screenshot/window, and brand token; do not encode a story in background decoration.
Keep problem statements sequential and hide their prior beat before the next resolves. The product preview must receive its own readable hold.
```js
timeline.to(generateButton, { scale: 0.86, repeat: 1, yoyo: true, duration: 0.12 }, submitAt);
```

Verify no particle or glow overlaps text, shell faces share a centre, and the final CTA has no card chrome.
